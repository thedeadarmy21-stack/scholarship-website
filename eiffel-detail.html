Eiffel Excellence Scholarship 2027

Last Date: 25 Jan, 2027
Degree: Master & PhD
Funding: Fully Funded

Description

The Eiffel Excellence Scholarship is a French government scholarship for international students in science, engineering, and mathematics. It covers tuition fees, living expenses, and travel costs for Master and PhD programs.

Eligibility Criteria

  • ✅ Pakistani students with strong academic record
  • ✅ Must be under 30 years of age
  • ✅ Science, engineering, or mathematics background
  • ✅ English or French language proficiency
