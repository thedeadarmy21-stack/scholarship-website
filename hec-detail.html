HEC Need Based Scholarship 2026

Last Date: 20 Nov, 2026
Degree: All Levels
Funding: Partially Funded

Description

The HEC Need Based Scholarship is a Higher Education Commission Pakistan scholarship for deserving students across Pakistan. It covers tuition fees and provides financial assistance to students from low-income families.

Eligibility Criteria

  • ✅ Pakistani students enrolled in HEC recognized universities
  • ✅ Must have financial need
  • ✅ Strong academic record
  • ✅ Must not have any other scholarship
