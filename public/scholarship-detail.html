Hungarian Government Scholarship 2027

Last Date: 15 Jan, 2027
Degree: All Levels
Funding: Fully Funded

Description

The Hungarian Government Scholarship (Stipendium Hungaricum) is a fully funded scholarship program for international students, including Pakistani students. It covers tuition fees, accommodation, living expenses, and health insurance for Bachelor, Master, and PhD programs at top Hungarian universities.

Eligibility Criteria

  • ✅ Pakistani students with 70%+ marks
  • ✅ Must be under 35 years of age
  • ✅ Strong academic record
  • ✅ English language proficiency (IELTS/TOEFL)
