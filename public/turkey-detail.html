Turkey Burslari Scholarship 2027

Last Date: 10 Feb, 2027
Degree: All Levels
Funding: Fully Funded

Description

Turkey Burslari Scholarship is a Turkish government full scholarship for Pakistani students at Turkish universities. It covers tuition fees, accommodation, living expenses, and health insurance for Bachelor, Master, and PhD programs.

Eligibility Criteria

  • ✅ Pakistani students with 70%+ marks
  • ✅ Must be under 30 years of age (Bachelor) or 35 (Master/PhD)
  • ✅ Strong academic record
  • ✅ English or Turkish language proficiency
