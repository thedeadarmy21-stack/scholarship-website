DAAD Germany Scholarship 2026

Last Date: 30 Dec, 2026
Degree: Master & PhD
Funding: Fully Funded

Description

The DAAD Germany Scholarship is a fully funded scholarship program for Pakistani students to study in Germany. It covers tuition fees, living expenses, health insurance, and travel costs. Available for Master and PhD programs at top German universities.

Eligibility Criteria

  • ✅ Pakistani students with 16 years of education
  • ✅ Minimum 70% marks in last degree
  • ✅ Strong academic record
  • ✅ German or English language proficiency
