Fulbright Scholarship 2026

Last Date: 15 Oct, 2026
Degree: Master & PhD
Funding: Fully Funded

Description

The Fulbright Scholarship is a prestigious USA government funded program for Pakistani students. It covers full tuition fees, living stipend, health insurance, and airfare. Available for Master and PhD programs at top US universities.

Eligibility Criteria

  • ✅ Pakistani citizens with 16 years of education
  • ✅ Strong academic record
  • ✅ Must have leadership qualities
  • ✅ English language proficiency (IELTS/TOEFL)
